DO YOU HAVE QUESTIONS?

Hello! We are ready to answer all of your question!

What services does this website provide?

▼

We help you record your daily income and expenses, manage your finances, set budget targets, see real-time financial reports, and save toward goals.

How do I record an expense or income?

▼

Make sure you are logged in, open the Transaction menu, choose Add New Income or Add New Expense, fill in the details, then click Save.

How do I create an account?

▼

Click Get Started or the profile icon at the top right, then fill in the sign-up form with your email and password.

Can I edit or delete my saving goals?

▼

Yes. You can edit or delete your saving goals anytime from the "View Savings" page.

Is my financial data safe on this website?

▼

Your financial data is kept private. We use encryption and up-to-date security protocols to keep it safe.

Does it cost anything to use this service?

▼

Our basic service is free, so you can use every feature without worrying about the cost.
